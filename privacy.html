Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bloom Blocks (the “App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use the Bloom Blocks mobile game.

Summary

  • Bloom Blocks does not require an account.
  • Bloom Blocks does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment details.
  • Game settings and progress such as high score, difficulty, board size, theme, shuffle count, and advertising preference are stored locally on your device.
  • The App may show optional ads through Yandex Mobile Ads. Yandex may process device, advertising, and usage data according to its own advertising technology policies.

Information stored by the App

Bloom Blocks stores the following data locally on your device using platform storage:

  • High score and game progress preferences.
  • Selected difficulty, board size, theme, and remaining shuffles.
  • Your advertising mode preference, such as no ads, bottom banner only, or gentle game-over ads.

This local data is used only to make the game work and remember your choices. We do not operate a user account system or our own game backend for this data.

Advertising

Bloom Blocks may use Yandex Mobile Ads to display ads, including banner ads, interstitial ads after game over, and optional rewarded ads for continuing a game. Ads are not shown during active gameplay in a way intended to interrupt play.

When ads are enabled, Yandex and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate technical information, ad interaction data, and other data needed to deliver, measure, prevent fraud, and improve ads. This processing is controlled by Yandex and/or its partners under their own policies.

For more information, review Yandex’s privacy and advertising information:

  • Yandex Privacy Policy
  • Yandex Mobile Ads SDK terms

Children

Bloom Blocks is a general-audience casual puzzle game and is not directed specifically to children under the age where parental consent is required by applicable law. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us through the support page.

Permissions

Bloom Blocks does not require access to sensitive device features such as contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location for core gameplay. Advertising SDKs may require network access to load ads.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data only as needed to operate the App, comply with law, protect rights and safety, or through third-party services integrated into the App such as Yandex Mobile Ads when ads are enabled.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App. Because Bloom Blocks currently does not operate user accounts, we cannot identify or delete local device data remotely.

Your choices

  • You can choose an advertising mode inside the App settings where available.
  • You can reset local data by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App.
  • You may be able to reset or limit your device advertising identifier in your operating system settings.

International transfers

Advertising and platform service providers may process data in countries other than your country of residence. Those providers are responsible for their own processing according to their policies and applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, please use the support page: Bloom Blocks Support.
